About this tool

What makes it “smart”

Nothing on this site is typed twice. Each issued timetable is entered once as data; every view below is derived from it when the page loads.

01

It knows where you are in the term

Start and end dates drive a live term progress bar, the week number, and a “right now / up next” panel that highlights the running session in the grid. A semester that has not started yet says so rather than showing a misleading “today”.

02

It audits itself against the specifications

Every timetable is checked against the approved scheme: which sanctioned courses actually appear on the grid, how many contact hours each one receives per week, and how that compares with its credit weighting. Gaps are listed, not hidden.

03

It catches clashes between programmes

MPH and MHA cohorts share classrooms and faculty. The site compares every session in every published timetable and flags overlaps in clock time where the venue or the named teacher is the same — including across the two programmes.

Source of truth: the grids reproduce the timetables issued by the School of Public Health. Course codes, titles and credits come from the MPH Programme & Course Specifications 2022 (Approved) and the MHA Programme Structure & Course Details 2022–2024. Where a timetable entry is ambiguous or a field is blank in the source, it is flagged on the Insights & Checks page rather than filled in by guesswork.
